I'm a tenure-track Assistant Professor at the Information Systems Technology and Design (ISTD) Pillar, Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD), where I hold the SUTD Assistant Professorship (SAP) research award. I'm also a Singapore National Research Foundation (NRF) Fellow (Class of 2026). Before joining SUTD, I was a research scientist at Alibaba Group Singapore. I obtained my Ph.D. from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Wai Lam.

My research spans natural language processing, large language models, large audio-language models, and LLM agents. I lead the iNLP Lab, where we work toward AI systems that stay reliable and efficient for diverse people, languages, and real-world settings. Much of that has centered on multilingual and multicultural LLMs, and increasingly on collective intelligence: how AI agents behave, coordinate, and evolve when they interact with each other and with people. Along the way, we've released open models and benchmarks widely used by the community, such as SeaLLMs, Babel, M3Exam, and DR-Arena.

My work has been featured in the AAAI New Faculty Highlight and received a SAC Highlight award at ACL 2026. I've also received the Alistar (阿里星) award (with Alibaba), named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia 2026 list, and recognized among the World's Top 2% Scientists.
